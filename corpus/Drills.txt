VENTRIXX TRAINING DRILLS

DRILL 1: Silent Entry
- Enter room
- No speech for 2 minutes
- Maintain presence

DRILL 2: 30-Second Balloon Build
- Create animal under time constraint
- Maintain eye contact

DRILL 3: Disruption Walk
- Walk through group
- Introduce unpredictable behavior
- No physical contact

DRILL 4: Mirror Conditioning
- Maintain expression under self-observation
- No laughter, no break

DRILL 5: Stress Simulation
- Loud environment
- Multiple observers
- Continue balloon construction

Scoring:
- Composure: 0–10
- Precision: 0–10
- Psychological impact: 0–10
