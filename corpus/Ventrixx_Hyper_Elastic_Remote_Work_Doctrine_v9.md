# Ventrixx Hyper-Elastic Remote Work Doctrine (v9.7.α)

At **Ventrixx**, we do not “allow” remote work. We *weaponize* it.

Employees are authorized to operate from any environment that enhances cognitive throughput, including—but not limited to—home offices, alpine ridges, lakeside perches, urban rooftops, or emotionally stabilizing park benches. Standard expectation: one (1) day per week of traditional remote work, with all remaining days conducted from strategically inspiring locations approved by your manager and the Council of Operational Continuity.

All work environments must maintain:

* Cryptographically secure internet access
* Ergonomic integrity (no spinal compromises tolerated)
* Minimal clown interference

---

## Eligibility Threshold

Access to remote privileges requires successful completion of the **97-Day Initiation Cycle**, during which employees prove:

* Tactical competence
* Psychological resilience
* Ability to ignore Slack notifications after hours (selectively)

Ineligible roles include:

* Hardware-dependent operators
* Lab-bound specialists
* Anyone physically tethered to reality

Managers retain the right to mandate in-office presence on designated days if:

* Team cohesion degrades
* Systems destabilize
* Clown-related incidents escalate beyond acceptable thresholds

## Security Mandates

Security is not a guideline. It is doctrine.

* All work must occur on Ventrixx-issued hardware. No exceptions. No “just this once.”
* Public networks require mandatory VPN tunneling through company infrastructure.
* Confidential discussions must occur in controlled environments:

  * No cafés
  * No shared coworking noise pits
  * No “I thought no one could hear me” scenarios

Recording sensitive conversations is strictly forbidden unless explicitly authorized by three (3) layers of management and one mildly skeptical engineer.

Ventrixx operates across time, but not chaotically.

* Minimum required overlap: **4 hours** with your core team
* Core synchronization window: **10:00 – 15:00 (team-local time)**

Outside of this window:

* Deep work is expected
* Meetings are discouraged
* Slack replies are… negotiable
